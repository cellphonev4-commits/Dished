The idea behind dished

Most recipe sites assume everyone at the table can eat everything. dished starts from the opposite assumption.

Find

Search by dish, ingredient, tag or cook, then filter the feed down to what you can actually eat.

Adapt

Pick an allergy and the ingredient list rewrites itself, with every swap marked so you can see the change.

Cook

Add the ingredients to your basket, flip to metric if you need it, tick off the steps as you go.
