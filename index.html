Why you'll love dished

Built for food lovers, home chefs, and anyone who has ever had to ask “can I actually eat this?”

Allergy & dietary customiser

One tap rewrites the ingredient list for dairy, gluten, nuts, peanuts, eggs, soy, shellfish and vegan swaps, with the substitution highlighted.

Share your recipes
